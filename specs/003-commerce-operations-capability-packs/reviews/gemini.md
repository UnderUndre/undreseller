## Alternative approaches considered

- **Separate `AppointmentBuffer` rows vs buffered `SlotReservation.startTime`/`endTime` + `appointmentStartTime`/`appointmentEndTime`**: Storing `appointmentStartTime`/`appointmentEndTime` alongside `bufferBeforeMin`/`bufferAfterMin` on `SlotReservation` while setting `startTime`/`endTime` to the full occupied interval lets the existing PostgreSQL GiST exclusion constraint `slot_resource_no_overlap` enforce both service buffers and outcall travel buffers atomically without extra join tables or custom range triggers.

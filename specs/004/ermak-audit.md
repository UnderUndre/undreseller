**Objective:** Identify the single primary operational bottleneck (TOC) before proposing automation, AI agents, CRM migration, custom software, or Operations OS initiatives.

---

# Executive Summary

This audit is intentionally solution-agnostic.

At this stage the objective is NOT to:

- implement AI
- deploy a new CRM
- build an Operations OS
- migrate systems
- automate processes

The objective is to identify:

1. Where money leaks
2. Where cases stall
3. Which activities consume management attention
4. Which workflows generate the most rework
5. Which single bottleneck limits company growth

Only after bottleneck discovery should a technical specification be created.

# Audit Success Criteria

The audit is successful only if:

- One primary constraint is identified
- At least 80% of workflow steps are mapped
- Main sources of operational waste are quantified
- One pilot feature can be justified economically
- A technical specification can be written from evidence, not assumptions

If these conditions are not met, software specifications are prohibited until more operational evidence is collected.

## Summary

The revision closes zcode's concrete defects well: range exclusion instead of a row per slot, one state machine, durable-ack intent, FTS-only retrieval, an admin write path, the DSN split and an honest RPO. What it did not check is the ground and the platforms: the `undreseller` root is BoxyHQ `saas-starter-kit` (Pages Router, next-auth middleware, anti-framing headers), which breaks every planned route and is also claimed by spec 002; Telegram Stars is scoped to digital goods while this catalog sells stocked products and in-person slots; Inngest runs functions inside the 500 MB container; and both named LLMs are retired. Fix the home and the rails first; most HIGH items are cheap before the create-only migration and expensive after the first client goes live.
